About Conference

The International Conference on Emerging Technologies, Innovation & Interdisciplinary Research 2026 is a premier global platform that brings together academicians, researchers, scientists, healthcare professionals, innovators and students.

The conference focuses on Artificial Intelligence, IoT, Blockchain, Data Science, Sustainable Innovation and interdisciplinary research to solve real-world challenges.

The event promotes knowledge sharing, collaboration, innovation and future-focused research discussions.

About Bhopal

Known as the "City of Lakes", Bhopal is the capital city of Madhya Pradesh and one of India’s fastest-growing educational, cultural, and technological hubs.

The city beautifully blends heritage with modern development and provides an ideal environment for international conferences, academic excellence, and global collaborations.

About SAM Global University

SAM Global University MP’s 1st AI Enable University, is a multidisciplinary institution committed to excellence in education, research, innovation, and holistic development.

Located in the serene environment of Raisen near Bhopal, the university provides world-class infrastructure, modern laboratories, experienced faculty, and strong industry-academia collaborations.

SAM Global University continuously promotes innovation, entrepreneurship, research culture, and skill-based education to prepare students for global opportunities.
